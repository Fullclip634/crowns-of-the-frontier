# Healing and repairs

Healers restore living troops. They do not heal mechanical siege engines, ships, buildings, or embarked passengers. The previous healer target filter accepted every unit, which allowed a nearby healer to refill a damaged battering ram while it attacked a castle. Attacking itself grants no health. Age and armor research continue to preserve the current health percentage when maximum health increases.

Villagers repair damaged castles and other completed friendly buildings. Select a villager and right-click the target on desktop, or tap it on a phone. Castles, towers, walls, gates, and monuments consume stone; ordinary building repairs consume timber. Repairs pause when resources run out. Stop or another worker command interrupts the job.

The same command repairs friendly rams, trebuchets, and ships. Mechanical repairs restore 23 health per second per working villager and cost 35% of the unit's training resources for a full health bar; partial damage costs proportionally less. The final tick is clamped to the missing health. An allied repair charges the repairing worker's commander. A damaged transport is repaired when the selected units are villagers; other troops can still board it, and villagers can board a full-health transport. Ships must be within the worker's reachable shore range.

Worker repairs for mechanical siege follow the distinction described in Blizzard's [official siege-unit guide](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/siegeunits.shtml); its [building guide](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/buildings.shtml) documents the 35% repair-cost reference. The repair rate and interface are this game's own rules.

`tests/maintenance.test.js` reproduces a ram attacking a castle beside a healer, checks automatic and explicit healing filters, actual resource spending, allied ownership, stopped jobs, saved jobs, and transport commands. The authoritative multiplayer test checks that a commander cannot borrow another player's worker, that allied repair costs are charged correctly, and that network healer orders cannot refill a ram. `scripts/maintenance-qa.mjs` exercises real desktop right-click and phone tap commands in the built game, then verifies the resulting repair, resource use, work animation, and painted battlefield. Browser phone emulation does not prove physical-device performance or every full match.
