All files in /audio/ are original waveforms synthesised by
scripts/author-audio-samples.mjs for Crowns of the Frontier.
No third-party recordings, samples or libraries were used. The author
dedicates these generated audio files to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The runtime keeps a procedural Web Audio fallback, so the game remains
fully playable when the sample pack cannot be fetched or decoded.
