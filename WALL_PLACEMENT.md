# Wall placement and camera controls

Select a villager, open **Build**, and choose **Wall**. Tap a start and an end, or drag a line across the battlefield. The solid 3D preview shows each proposed section. Green means the section can be built, red marks blocked ground, and gold marks sections beyond the current resource budget. The toolbar shows the section count and stone cost. Nothing is ordered or paid for until **Build wall** is pressed. A single start tap can be confirmed as one section.

While placing walls, dragging the battlefield adjusts the line and leaves the camera still. Choose **Move map** to drag the camera, then **Place wall** to resume drawing. The square minimap, camera keys, and desktop middle mouse drag can also move the view. Two-finger pinch zooms without rotating. **Reset** clears only the draft; **Cancel** or **Done** closes the tool. On desktop, right-click and Esc also cancel placement. After a successful order the tool stays open with an empty draft so another line can be started deliberately. Previously ordered workers continue building the sections, including separate lines and individual walls; Stop or a new unit order interrupts that work.

On a small landscape phone, the placement toolbar keeps 44-pixel touch buttons at the top, temporarily hides the selection and command panels, and leaves the minimap available. The panels return when the tool closes. Ordinary one-finger dragging pans the camera outside wall placement. Notifications sit below the toolbar rather than over its buttons.

Preview and confirmation use the existing simulation's wall spacing, age, budget, visibility, and construction validation. The planner connects to an existing owned wall at either endpoint and does not pay for a duplicate end section. A blocked or unaffordable line cannot be confirmed. Multiplayer requests continue through the authoritative server, which checks the final order when it arrives; a world change between preview and confirmation can still make that request fail. Destruction leaves a real breach; the placement tool does not close gaps automatically.

The interaction follows the end-to-end construction and wall-footprint clarity described in the official [Age of Empires IV wall update](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/age-of-empires-iv-update-9-1-109/). Tap endpoints, a separate confirmation button, and explicit Move map are this game's touch controls.

Validation: `node --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/wall-placement.test.js tests/walls.test.js` checks actual paid sections, endpoint links, blockers, budgets, and preview geometry. `node .tools/run-wall-tool-checks.mjs` runs the full suite and production build. The owned browser checks in `scripts/wall-placement-qa.mjs` exercise actual mouse and touch gestures, native WebGPU and its Compatibility Mode, classic WebGL, and painted desktop and landscape-phone frames. Emulated touch layouts and browser GPU checks do not measure physical-phone frame rate or prove every complete match.
