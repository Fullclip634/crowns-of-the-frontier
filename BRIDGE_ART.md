# River bridge artwork

The original stone bridge is authored with the installed Blender 4.5 LTS from `scripts/blender/author-bridge.py`. It has three barrel vaults with solid wedge stones, full piers and pointed cutwaters, staggered paving, bonded parapets, beveled coping and sparse moss. The generated source is self-contained; rebuilding does not require an existing editable scene or a downloaded model.

Run `npm run art:bridge`. It writes the editable `.blend` under `.tools/art-candidate/bridge/raw/` and the optimized GLB plus metadata under `.tools/art-candidate/bridge/full/`. `BLENDER_BIN` and `GLTFPACK_BIN` can point to installed tools in another workspace. After reviewing the output, copy those two files into `src/assets3d/`. The checked-in asset is 204,560 bytes and 9,728 triangles. Runtime bank aprons bring each crossing to 11,456 triangles in three material batches. All graphics presets use the same silhouette and road geometry; their normal surface and lighting settings still apply.

The rendered riverbed continues under the main arch. `Landscape.walkableHeightAt` supplies the raised road and bank-ramp heights to unit placement and ground commands. A terrain clearance collar keeps even the coarsest supported ground triangles below the paving. Generated resources and cosmetic ground clutter leave the crossing clear, and new buildings cannot overlap its masonry. On restoring older saves, resources planted on the crossing move to a clear bank with their IDs and remaining supplies preserved; affected workers retain their gathering target and replan their route. The previous land navigation corridor is retained. Bridge visibility follows exploration.

Design references: [English Heritage's Moulton bridge history](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/moulton-packhorse-bridge/history/) describes masonry, parapets and the use of several arches to maintain a manageable road slope. [The Rochester Bridge Trust](https://rbt.org.uk/bridges/historical-bridges/the-medieval-bridge/) describes protective piers and cutwaters. [The official AoE IV Season One notes](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/age-of-empires-iv-season-one-update-release-notes/) discuss bridges as readable land crossings and strategic boundaries between waterways. These informed original artwork and crossing integration; they are not game assets or a claim of equivalent overall production quality.

`tests/bridge.test.js` checks actual optimized geometry against walking/command heights, open arches and solid piers, parapets, fresh resource placement, construction exclusions and ordinary army movement through both river maps. Rendered screenshots and release results are recorded separately; geometry counts do not certify device frame rate or the broader game goal.

The default `npm test` command runs one test file at a time because the Blender fixtures retain large decoded meshes. Multi-client scenarios still create their complete sets of connections.
