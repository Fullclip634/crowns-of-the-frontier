# Page zoom and battlefield camera controls

Double-tapping must not enlarge the entire page or push the game interface off-screen. This is separate from deliberately pinching the battlefield to change camera distance. The earlier implementation disabled the latter by mistake; camera pinch is restored, with no two-finger rotation. Camera buttons and ordinary desktop wheel input remain available.

The page declares a fixed-scale viewport and `touch-action: manipulation` on the document, controls and relevant scroll containers. Text inputs on touch devices use at least 16px text to avoid small-field focus zoom. Canvas and minimap retain their own `touch-action: none` gesture handling. Vertical dialog and selection-panel scrolling remain native.

CSS alone is insufficient on some iOS versions: WebKit documents [double-tap zoom ignoring manipulation](https://commits.webkit.org/317443@main). A non-passive capture listener therefore cancels only the second nearby, short, completed tap during a match, including paused dialogs. Drags, long presses, canceled touches and multi-touch reset its tap candidate. Editable fields retain their native focus and selection behavior. Menus outside a match retain normal touch behavior.

Canceling that touchend can also suppress its native click. The game preserves the action explicitly: HUD buttons already completed by pointerup are not clicked again; other buttons, links and disclosure summaries receive one click. Double-tapping a troop still selects nearby troops of that type. Multi-touch cancels pending orders and wall placement until the fingers are released while changing only the camera distance.

`tests/battle-zoom-guard.test.js` covers double-tap timing/distance, interruption by scrolling, long presses, canceled gestures and multi-touch, editable fields, inactive menus, listener removal and native zoom gestures/shortcuts. Production Chromium touch checks at 568 by 320 and 667 by 375 confirmed page scale 1, working camera pinch without rotation or unit orders, exactly two activations for two rapid taps on both HUD and dialog buttons, single-finger panning, deliberate camera buttons, 16px touch inputs and painted WebGL scenes without horizontal overflow or page errors. Duplicate compatibility clicks are intercepted in capture phase before direct widget listeners as well as delegated commands. These checks cannot establish physical old-iPhone Safari compatibility. Operating-system accessibility magnification is outside page control.

References: [WebKit's touch-action guidance](https://webkit.org/blog/5610/more-responsive-tapping-on-ios/), its [iOS double-tap correction](https://commits.webkit.org/317443@main), and Apple's [Safari gesture handling guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AppleApplications/Reference/SafariWebContent/HandlingEvents/HandlingEvents.html).
